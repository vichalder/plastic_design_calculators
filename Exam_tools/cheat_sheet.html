Injection Molding Rules of Thumb

Thermodynamics & Rheology

  • Viscosity Reduction: Introduce heat to increase molecular free volume, allowing chains to slip.
  • Shear-Thinning (Pseudoplasticity): Higher shear rates align macromolecular chains parallel to flow, dropping apparent viscosity.
  • Amorphous (e.g., PC-ABS): Random molecular structure. Gradual, linear shrinkage (~3% volumetric).
  • Semi-Crystalline (e.g., PP): Folds into dense lamellae. Severe, non-linear shrinkage (~6% volumetric).

Part Design & Defect Mitigation

  • Uniform Wall Thickness: Prevents differential cooling and warpage.
  • Gate Placement: ALWAYS locate at the thickest section to establish unidirectional flow to thinnest parts.
  • Radii over Sharp Corners: Sharp corners multiply stress (\(K_t > 3.0\)). Radii distribute force flow lines.
  • Ejector Marks: Caused by too short cooling times. Core yields under pin pressure while hot.
